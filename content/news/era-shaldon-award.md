Professor Michael Balzer has been awarded the **ERA Stanley Shaldon Award for Young Investigators in Translational Science** for his outstanding scientific work on new approaches to understanding kidney disease.

The award is presented by the **European Renal Association (ERA)** and recognizes exceptional achievements by young investigators in translational kidney research. Prof. Balzer’s work focuses on mechanisms of kidney adaptation, repair, and regeneration after acute and chronic injury, with a particular emphasis on single-cell technologies to identify disease-relevant cell states.

The award, endowed with €10,000, was presented during the **ERA Congress 2026**.
